## 原创范例

> Subject: Updated delivery schedule
>
> Dear Mr. Park,
>
> We regret to inform you that your shipment has been delayed due to bad weather. It is now expected to arrive on Tuesday. If this revised date is not convenient, please contact our service desk so that we can discuss an alternative arrangement.
>
> Best regards,
> Customer Service

## 中文理解

恶劣天气导致配送延迟，新预计日期为周二。若不方便，收件人应联系服务台讨论替代安排。

## 结构与语气

inform 人 that 从句；due to + 名词短语；has been delayed 是现在完成时被动；expected 表示预计而不是保证。

## 阅读焦点

分清延迟原因、原计划是否已改变、新日期是否确定，以及下一步由谁采取行动。
