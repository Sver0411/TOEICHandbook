## 动词不是一个孤立词

request information、conduct a survey、submit a report 是三个不同阶段的动作。把动词和典型宾语一起记，才能知道什么动作发生在什么对象上。

## 人与物的排列方式

| 动词 | 结构 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| provide | 人 + with + 物 | provide guests with breakfast |
| require | 人 + to do | require visitors to register |
| notify | 人 + of + 事 | notify staff of changes |
| replace | A + with + B | replace the old part with a new one |

## doing 与 to do 由搭配决定

recommend / suggest / avoid / postpone 后常接 doing；plan / agree / manage 后接 to do。介词后用名词或 doing，尤其留意 **look forward to working** 中 to 是介词。

## 三个容易用错的句子

**We recommend booking early.** 不写 recommend to book。**We arranged for a technician to visit.** for 不能遗漏。**She is responsible for checking inventory.** for 后用 checking。

## 场景化理解

> Please submit the report to the manager by Friday.
>
> 请在周五之前向经理提交报告。

the report 是提交对象，to the manager 是接收方，by Friday 是截止时间。一句话把搭配与实际信息一起表达。
